Taylor Miller is a writer and researcher based between the Sonoran and Chihuahuan Deserts. She earned her Ph.D. in political geography from the University of Arizona. Taylor is a contributing editor and writer for Columbia University’s *Avery Review*, and other recent works are featured with *Social Text*, *Al Mayadeen*, *Weird Economies*, *The New Inquiry*, *Protean*, with forthcoming essays in Cambridge University’s *Scroope*, Yale School of Architecture’s *Perspecta*, *Antipode* and *Kohl Journal*. She’s motivated by border abolition and cactus propagation.
