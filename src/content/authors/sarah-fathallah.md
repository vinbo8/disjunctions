Sarah Fathallah is a community organiser and an MSt candidate in AI Ethics and Society at the Leverhulme Centre for the Future of Intelligence, University of Cambridge. Their research explores the carceral impacts of surveillance technologies on colonial subjects, criminalised communities, migrants, and political dissenters, especially along racialised lines. Sarah also serves as a research fellow with _7amleh_, investigating Israel’s voice surveillance apparatus and its impacts on Palestinians.
