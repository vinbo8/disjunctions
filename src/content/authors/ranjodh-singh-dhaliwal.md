name: Ranjodh Singh Dhaliwal
---
Ranjodh Singh Dhaliwal studies the politicoeconomic and sociocultural entanglements of technological media through history. He is currently employed as a professor at the University of Basel. He is a co-author of "Neural Networks" (meson press and University of Minnesota Press, 2024) and the incoming president of the Society for Literature, Science, and the Arts (SLSA). His award-winning writing — situated between critical media theory, literary studies, computer science, critical design, and STS — can be found in *Critical Inquiry*, *Configurations*, *Social Text*, *American Literature*, the *Journal of Cinema and Media Studies*, ACM FDG, ACM UIST, *Design Issues*, and the *Frankfurter Allgemeine Zeitung*, amongst other scholarly and popular venues.
