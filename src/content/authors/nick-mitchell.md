Nick Mitchell writes on higher education and the politics of knowledge in the US. Her research can be found in essays published in *Feminist Studies*, *Critical Ethnic Studies*, *The New Inquiry*, and *Spectre*, as well as in two forthcoming books: "Discipline and Surplus: Black Studies at the Dawn of Neoliberalism" (under contract with Duke University Press), and "The University, in Theory: Essays on Institutionalized Knowledge". She works in the Critical Race and Ethnic Studies Department at UC Santa Cruz.
