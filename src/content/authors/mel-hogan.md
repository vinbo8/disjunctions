Mél Hogan is the host of [*The Data Fix*](thedatafix.net) podcast and editor of [*Heliotrope*](heliotropejournal.net). She is Associate Professor in Film & Media at Queen’s University, Canada. Her research focuses on data centres in the contexts of planetary catastrophes and collective anxieties about the future. You can follow her on Bluesky at @melhogan.bsky.social.
