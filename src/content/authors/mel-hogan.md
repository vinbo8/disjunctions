Mél Hogan (Bluesky: [@melhogan.bsky.social](https://bsky.app/profile/melhogan.bsky.social)) is the host of [*The Data Fix*](thedatafix.net) podcast and editor of [*Heliotrope*](heliotropejournal.net). She is Associate Professor in Film & Media at Queen’s University, Canada. Her research focuses on data centres in the contexts of planetary catastrophes and collective anxieties about the future.
